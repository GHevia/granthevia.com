How to play

  1. Tap an open square to cycle through empty, queen, and knight.
  2. Place exactly the number of pieces shown above the board.
  3. Every empty square must be attacked by at least one piece.
  4. No queen or knight may attack another piece.
How the pieces move

Queens attack along rows, columns, and diagonals until another piece blocks them.

Knights attack in an L shape: two squares in one direction and one to the side.
